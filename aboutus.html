Our Story

About Us

Deliverance Church International Nyansiongo is a Pentecostal church under Deliverance Church International Ministries, under the able leadership of Bishop Dr. Mark Kariuki and the Apostolic team.

The church was started in the year 2002 by Rev. Mr & Mrs. Barnabas and Margaret Mititi, who have steered it to date by the grace of God. The ministry has grown and led to the birth of three other branches in the South Nyanza East region. The church has grown in leaps and bounds, and in the process God has raised other pastors to help oversee different ministries in the church.

Sister Branches

  • Deliverance Church International Chepilat
  • Deliverance Church International Manga Scheme
  • Deliverance Church International Chepng'ombe
