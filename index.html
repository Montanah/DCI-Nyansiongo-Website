Life in our church

Moments of fellowship.

A look back at the gatherings that bring our church family together.

  • Past gathering

    Easter Convention

    Our annual Easter Convention took place from April 17th to 21st, 2025, with indoor sessions from 9:30am to 1:30pm in the church hall and outdoor sessions from 3:00pm at the Nyansiongo Town Council gardens. The ministers for the event were:

    • Rev. Gichiambati from Sirare
    • Rev. Wycliffe Omurwa from Narok
    • Ev. Nina Waryaro from Uganda
    • Min. Beatrice Moses from Kisii
    All are welcome to join us!

  • Past gathering

    Prayer Warriors' Network Mission

    A network of students from different polytechnics and colleges across the country being hosted by our church for a soul harvesting evangelism mission in conjunction with our church missioners.
    16th to 22nd December 2024.

  • Past gathering

    Easter Convention

    Our annual Easter Convention took place from the 28th to the 31st of March 2024, with indoor sessions from 9:00am to 1:00pm in the church hall and outdoor sessions from 2:00pm to 7:00pm at the Nyansiongo Town Council gardens. The ministers for the event were:

    • Pst. Innocent Omoke from Kisii
    • Pst. Richard Birir from Kericho
    • Evangelist Nina Waryaro from Uganda
    All are welcome to join us!

  • Past gathering

    Shiloh Prayer Festival

    Shiloh Prayer Festival is our annual first prayer and fasting week at the beginning of each year, to seek God's guidance and direction throughout the year. The 2024 festival took place from 8th – 14th January, 6am – 6pm. All are welcome to join us!
